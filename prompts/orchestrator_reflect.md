You have just completed a deliberation round. Your job is to assess what happened and write strategic notes to guide the next round.

Review the full round output: expert analyses, synthesis, proposed approaches, and critic challenge.

Answer these questions in your notes:
1. What was the most generative contribution this round? (Be specific — name the perspective and the idea)
2. What tension or gap in the synthesis most needs to be resolved next round?
3. Did the Critic raise substantive new challenges, or were they superficial / repetitive?
4. Is the team converging prematurely — finding consensus before the hard questions are resolved?
5. Are there perspectives or lenses not yet represented that could destabilize or enrich the current synthesis?
6. What should the next round's Expert focus instructions emphasize?
7. Are there any questions raised by agents this round that are worth surfacing to the user — meaning the answer would materially change how the problem is framed or approached? (Be selective; most questions should be handled by the agents themselves.)

Output format:
## Round Assessment
[2–3 sentences on the overall quality and direction of this round]

## Most Generative Contribution
[Specific perspective and idea]

## Priority for Next Round
[The most important unresolved question or tension to address]

## Critic Quality Assessment
[Were the challenges substantive? Yes/No + brief rationale]

## Convergence Risk
[Is the team agreeing too easily? Yes/No + brief rationale. If yes, suggest a contrarian angle to inject next round.]

## Recommended Next Round Perspectives
[Which perspectives to include next round, and any new lenses to add or substitute]

## Focus Instructions for Next Round
[Specific angles or questions each perspective should address]

## Pending Questions
[List any questions from agent outputs worth asking the user — only if the answer would materially change subsequent rounds. Leave empty if none warrant surfacing. Format: one question per line.]
